# Restaurant Catalogue App
Universal is the problem of revisiting a hometown diner that was once a regular stop, and forgetting the meal that made it one of your favorite. Too often I'd find myself at a restaurant I could remember having a great meal at a time before, but when I went to find it on the menu, I couldn't even recall a thing about it.

This app allows you to quickly document and rate restaurants and their dishes so you never forget why your experience was so amazing years down the line.

* Re-download ExpoGo on iPhone
* Run `npx expo start -c`
* Scan QR code from terminal with phone (redirects to ExpoGo)
* View app
## Homepage
### Buttons
* Restaurants - lists all restaurants and gives option to create new ones
* Dishes - lists all dishes and gives option to create new ones
* Dinining Entries - lists all dining entries and gives option to create new ones
* Dining statistics - currently barebone and not powered by any real numbers
